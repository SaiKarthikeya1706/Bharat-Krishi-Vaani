# Pitch deck outline — 11 slides

1. **Title** — Bharat Krishi Vaani. One line: "An AI crop advisor a farmer can talk to."
2. **The problem** — small/marginal farmers rely on guesswork over data; crop
   loss and food security risk; no shared digital layer across states.
3. **Who feels this** — a specific farmer persona: which state, which crop,
   what a bad season costs them. Make it concrete, not abstract.
4. **The idea, in one flow** — photo/voice in → Gemini diagnosis → spoken
   advisory in their language → pin on a shared map. (Screenshot the app.)
5. **Live demo** — either embed a clip here or say "see demo video" and move on.
6. **Where Google AI does real work** — Gemini multimodal reads the photo,
   reasons over local agronomy context, and writes the advisory directly in
   the farmer's language — name the model, be specific, judges are scoring this.
7. **Why voice + multilingual matters here** — literacy and smartphone-literacy
   gaps; this is the difference between a tool farmers can use and one they can't.
8. **The map layer** — every report becomes a data point; hotspots emerge
   without anyone manually aggregating them — this is the seed of the
   demand-aggregation layer the broader governance problem needs.
9. **Scaling across India** — new state = new grounding data; new language =
   one line of code; architecture doesn't change. Show the concrete diff.
10. **Deployability** — what it would take to pilot with an actual PHC/krishi
    vigyan kendra network in weeks, not months: what exists today vs. what's left.
11. **Ask / close** — what you'd build next with more time (real dataset
    integration, offline/SMS fallback for low-connectivity areas, etc).

Keep each slide to one idea. Screenshots of the running app do more work than
text — use them on slides 4, 5, and 8.
